## What Nexus collects, and why

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your Minecraft username and UUID | When you sign in to Nexus online features | To identify you to friends, clans and parties |
| Friends, blocks, friend requests, direct messages, clan chat | When you use those features | To provide them; messages are kept so you can read them later |
| Which server you're playing on | While Nexus is open (you can hide it: Settings → "show my server") | So friends can see and join you |
| Playtime and a once-a-minute activity summary in-game (movement, turning, key and click counts – **not** what you type) | While playing with a clan | To award clan honor and detect AFK players |
| Reports you send about other players, and reports about you | When someone reports | Safety and moderation |
| Crash reports (your PC's hardware, Windows/Mac/Linux version, mod list, end of the game log) | **Only if you choose** (Settings → Crash reports, or "Send to Nexus support") | To fix crashes |
| Share codes you create | When you create one | So others can use your setup |
| Cosmetics you wear | When you choose one | To show them to other Nexus players |

## What Nexus never collects
- Your Microsoft password. Sign-in happens on Microsoft's own page.
- Your Microsoft or Minecraft sign-in tokens. They stay on your computer, encrypted by your operating
  system (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service).
- The contents of your worlds, screenshots or clips. They stay on your computer.
